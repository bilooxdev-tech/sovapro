SovaPro Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SovaPro handles information when you use the bot.

Information We Collect

SovaPro may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames and information required for the bot's features.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide currency, economy, rewards and utility features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user information for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Some information may be stored when required for features such as balances, rewards and server settings.

Data Removal

If you want to request the removal of your stored information, contact the SovaPro support team.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the SovaPro support team.
